EmergiSense

An ambulance escort system for traffic signals: it hears the siren, confirms the ambulance on camera, and switches the signal so the ambulance never waits at a red light.

98%detection accuracy
30+ semergency response time saved
Edgereal-time on Jetson Nano
  • Audio first. 16 kHz microphone audio is cut into 2-second windows with a 1-second hop, turned into log-spectrograms and classified by a 1-D CNN as siren or not.
  • The siren wakes the camera. An object detector trained on Indian ambulances confirms the vehicle at a score of 0.95 or more, and OCR on the mirrored frame reads the reversed AMBULANCE lettering on the bonnet.
  • Both must agree. Only then does the Jetson Nano drive an Arduino pin to switch the signal, so a siren alone can't change the lights.
  • Runs in real time on an NVIDIA Jetson Nano with a webcam and microphone. Led the team to Rank 1 nationwide at the Smart India Hackathon; pitched to Maharashtra Police, AICTE and the BHAU Institute, COEP Pune. One national patent issued.
